Physical sense: ESP32 GPIO controls/reads a 5-channel relay module; relay contacts switch the AC line to each fan/light while the microcontroller remains low-voltage.
Current sensing: An ACS712/CT clamp can sit on the room supply line to estimate total room load, then backend maps watts to each known device rating.
Demo scope: One room is enough for Wokwi/Tinkercad; repeat the same relay + sensor block for the other rooms.
